Welcome To JB Hi-Fi

Established in 1975, JB Hi-Fi began as a retailer in East Keilor, Victoria, Australia with one simple philosophy: to deliver a specialist range of hi-fi and recorded music at Australia's lowest prices.

Now, maintaining this philosophy, JB is Australia's leading independent retailer of home entertainment and recorded music with 11 superstores throughout Victoria and New South Wales. JB offers the world's leading brands in hi-fi, speakers, televisions, VCRs, car sound, home theatre and portable audio and continues to stock an exclusive range of specialist hi-fi. JB also offers Australia's largest range of recorded music, with over 150,000 CD titles, all at Australia's lowest prices! JB has it all, huge range, cheapest prices, convenient locations, but most importantly genuine personal service from our expert experienced staff. So browse our site to keep up to date with the latest music news, product information and many great offers.

About JB Hi-Fi

JB Hi-Fi is Australia's largest home entertainment retailer, built on top products, great value and the same specialist-store energy that began in East Keilor.

The current About Us page celebrates 50 years of JB with a record-sleeve style history image, which feels suspiciously perfect for this retro build. The new site has grown into TVs, audio, computers, gaming, mobile, movies, music, appliances, smart home, services and a national store network, but the loud yellow retail heart is still doing the heavy lifting.
